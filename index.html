The Challenge

When Old Concrete Cannot Keep Up With Memphis Living

Cracked driveways, uneven patio slabs, and sinking walkways do more than hurt curb appeal in Memphis. They create trip hazards, channel rainwater toward foundations, and leave outdoor spaces unusable when weather shifts. Many properties across Midtown, Cooper-Young, and East Memphis were poured years ago over unstable fill with finishes that never accounted for humid heat, clay soil, and sudden stormwater.

Property owners across Mid-South also need flatwork that drains correctly and holds up under real use. A heaving section near a side entry or a deteriorating walk at a rental property creates safety concerns and repeat repairs that proper planning could have prevented.

Whether you need a wider driveway, a patio refresh, a pool deck replacement, or a new walk connecting the garage to the backyard, concrete should be planned for your lot, not poured as a generic patch that fails within a few seasons.

Concrete installation in Memphis TN covers a wide range of needs. Some homeowners want a driveway replacement after years of edge breakdown. Others need a patio with steps to a pool deck and a walkway from the side gate to the front entry. Each scenario calls for different thickness, reinforcement, joint layout, and finish.

Driveways remain a common request across Memphis because they take daily abuse from vehicles, delivery trucks, and weather exposure. Wider layouts accommodate multiple cars and trailers common in neighborhoods throughout Shelby County. Proper base material and compaction matter where Mississippi River valley clay with soft spots on older properties.

Patios give homeowners clean entertaining space without constant upkeep. Broom finishes add grip around grills and wet areas. Stamped and colored concrete adds texture at entry points and rear yards where plain gray slab would feel unfinished against local architecture.

Walkways connect driveways to front doors, side yards, and garden areas. Even short sections need correct pitch and stable edges so they do not chip or tilt after storms. On sloped lots around Memphis, we follow grade changes without awkward steps that collect debris.

Pool deck concrete must account for splash-out, furniture placement, and drainage away from the pool shell. We plan expansion joints, coping transitions, and surface texture so the deck stays comfortable underfoot and directs water where it belongs.

Epoxy flooring refreshes garages, basements, and utility spaces where bare concrete stains and dusts. Coatings add durability, easier cleanup, and a finished look that complements newer Memphis homes and renovated storage areas.

Stamped concrete gives the look of stone or brick without individual pavers shifting after soil movement. We apply patterns and sealers suited to humid heat, clay soil, and sudden stormwater so color and texture hold up through heat, rain, salt, and sun exposure common across Mid-South.

Removal and replacement of existing concrete is often part of a Memphis project. Old slabs may need to come out before new base material goes in. We handle breaking, hauling, and disposal so you are not coordinating multiple vendors for one upgrade.

FAQ

Common Questions About Concrete Work in Memphis TN

How long does a typical concrete project take in Memphis?
Most residential driveways, patios, and walkways take two to five days depending on square footage, removal of existing concrete, and weather during cure time. Larger pool deck or stamped projects may take longer. We provide a schedule during your estimate so you know when crews will be on site.
Do I need a permit for new concrete in Memphis?
Permit requirements depend on project type, location on the lot, and local rules in Shelby County. We help you understand what applies during the consultation and can advise on documentation when a permit is required.
Which concrete finish works best for Memphis weather?
Broom finishes offer grip on sloped areas and wet surfaces. Smooth finishes suit covered patios. Stamped concrete adds texture and color with periodic sealing. Epoxy works well for garage interiors. We recommend options based on sun exposure, drainage, intended use, and maintenance preferences.
Can you repair or replace sections of existing concrete?
Yes. We remove and replace cracked driveways, sunken patio sections, damaged pool deck areas, and worn walkways. Call (901) 698-9734 to describe the damage and we will recommend repair or full replacement.
